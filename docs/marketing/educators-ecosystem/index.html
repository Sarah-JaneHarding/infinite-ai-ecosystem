04 · ENTERPRISE-GRADE ARCHITECTURE

Unbending rules of trust.

At the frontier of AI in education, trust isn't assumed — it's engineered, with strict guardrails that don't bend for convenience.

AI drafts. The teacher decides. At the heart of the system sits a single shared memory — every CAPS document, every DBE Annual Teaching Plan, and a school's own policies, planted as ground truth. Nothing is hallucinated or forgotten. The teacher remains the final authority.
Unbending Rule 01

POPIA Safe & Local

A dedicated privacy layer keeps every child's personal information detached from the core AI models. Sensitive data is designed to stay encrypted and hosted in South Africa (AWS af-south-1).

Unbending Rule 02

Human-in-the-Loop

A human being — the teacher, the HoD, the support team — must explicitly approve, edit or reject what the system drafts before it ever reaches a classroom.

Unbending Rule 03

Immutable Audit Trails

Every AI generation and human decision is dated, remembered and reversible. A school can trace the exact origin of any lesson plan or rubric with full transparency.

Unbending Rule 04

Bias-Checked, Guardrailed Growth

The system refines itself strictly from verified human corrections, never assumptions — improvement that stays equitable and grounded in real, supplied source material.
