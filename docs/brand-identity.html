Brand Identity System
Origin

Built for memory
that doesn't end

South Africa's classrooms carry knowledge that disappears. A teacher retires and twenty years of hard-won insight on teaching isiZulu phonics walks out the door. A learner is screened for support, assessed, referred — and three years later the cycle begins again because no one can find the file. PIRLS 2021 told us 81% of Grade 4 learners cannot read for meaning in any language. That is not a technology problem. It is a memory problem.

The Infinite Brain was conceived as the answer: not an AI that replaces the people in these rooms, but one that ensures nothing they know is ever lost again. A living institutional memory that grows with the school, defers to the teacher at every decision point, and holds the evidence of every choice made for every child.

The name is the promise. As long as the ecosystem breathes, the knowledge doesn't end.

"Fix the soil, not the buckets." — Founding product principle

The Mark

The Lemniscate

A single unbroken curve — no start, no end. Every loop feeds the other. Every journey through the mark crosses the same convergence point, where memory and action meet.

The Arc

A continuous cubic bezier path that never intersects itself at the outer edge — only at the centre crossing. Violet originates in the left loop; cyan completes the right. The gradient is motion, not decoration.

The Convergence

At coordinates (32, 16) in the mark's coordinate space — the exact centre — both loops cross. This point represents the meeting of AI suggestion and human decision. It is the most structurally significant pixel in the system.

Clear Space

Reserve a minimum equal to the mark's height on all four sides. Never place text, other marks, or images inside this boundary. Minimum display width: 80px. Below that, use the wordmark alone.

Voice & Tone

Plain, warm, honest.

Write for the teacher reading this at 6am on a Tuesday who has not yet had coffee and has thirty things to do before the bell. Plain does not mean simple. Honest does not mean cold.

Warm ↔ Clinical

WarmClinical

Respect educators as professionals. Never patronise. Never over-explain.

Plain ↔ Technical

PlainTechnical

Use the educator's vocabulary, not the engineer's. Explain jargon the first time, then trust the reader.

Hopeful ↔ Cautious

HopefulCautious

Ground every claim. PIRLS 2021. CAPS clause. SIAS step number. Hope without evidence is hype.

Permanent ↔ Adaptive

PermanentAdaptive

The memory endures. The approach evolves. These are not in tension — they are the same promise.

Name things by what they do

The Brain remembers. The Engine plans. The Toolbox makes. Write the verb, not the noun.

AI suggests. The teacher decides.

Every agent output is a draft. Every ratification is a human act. Never imply otherwise — not in marketing, not in UI copy, not in reports.

Cite the evidence

PIRLS 2021. CAPS section. SIAS process step. If a claim can't be cited, it isn't made.

Use the ecosystem language

Soil. Roots. Seasons. Habitats. Harvest. The metaphor is structural — it maps to the architecture. Prefer it to abstract tech-speak.

Never claim learner outcomes

We do not yet have a published randomised evaluation. Until we do, we claim process quality, evidence-grounding, and time saved — not reading scores.

✓ Write like this

"Your lesson plan is ready for review — you'll want to check the assessment weighting on Activity 3."

"This learner has been at Tier 2 for 6 weeks. The SBST meeting is due."

"81% of Grade 4 learners in SA cannot read for meaning. This is what we're here to change — carefully."

✗ Not like this

"AI-powered learning transformation for the 21st century educator!"

"Our revolutionary platform uses cutting-edge machine learning to boost reading scores."

"Replace repetitive tasks with intelligent automation."

Motion Language

Flow, not flash.

Continuous

The lemniscate never stops. Brand animations loop without a perceptible seam — no bounce-in, no flash, no sudden stops. The loop is the proof of the promise.

Breathing

Glow intensity, opacity, scale — these pulse at 0.8–1.2 Hz, matching a calm resting breath. Never faster. Fast animation signals urgency; this system signals patience.

Gradient-directional

Particles travel violet → cyan, always. Counter-flow is never used decoratively. When two streams meet at the convergence point, that is the brand's most charged moment.

Reduced motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. The static mark is always sufficient. Motion enhances; it never carries essential information.
