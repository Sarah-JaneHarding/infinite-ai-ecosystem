Instructions and Information for Markers

1
Objective Marking

Marking must be strictly objective. Give full credit for any alternative, relevant, and well-motivated insight that fits the context of the question.

2
No Double Penalties

Avoid penalising a learner twice for the exact same systematic error (e.g. an early calculation error followed by correct subsequent methods).

3
Exceeding Word / Length Constraints

Do not penalise candidates simply for exceeding suggested lengths (e.g. in essays or open-ended responses) unless the active assessment rubric specifically requires it.

4
Language and Spelling

Focus on the core content and understanding. Minor spelling mistakes should not cost marks unless they change the grammatical meaning or impact specific language-testing outcomes.

5
Tick Allocation

Place a clear, visible tick (✓) next to every factual point, method step, or cognitive skill that earns a mark.
